REF LOGO KIT — by unfayr

FOLDERS
  svg/icon      ref-icon.svg (full colour), ref-icon-black.svg, ref-icon-white.svg
  svg/lockup    ref-lockup.svg            full colour, for white/light backgrounds
                ref-lockup-reversed.svg   full colour, for black/dark backgrounds
                ref-lockup-black.svg      one colour, black
                ref-lockup-white.svg      one colour, white
  png/icon      full-colour icon at 1024, 512, 256, 180, 64, 48, 32, 16 px;
                one-colour icons at 1024 and 512 px
  png/lockup    every lockup at 2000 px and 1000 px wide
  favicon       favicon.ico (16/32/48), favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png (180)

All SVGs are pure vector with text converted to outlines: no fonts needed.
All PNGs have transparent backgrounds.

COLOURS
  Ultra   #1E1BF5   the icon tile
  Ink     #0A0A0A   stripes, wordmark on light
  White   #FFFFFF   stripes, wordmark on dark

TYPE
  "ref"        Space Grotesk Bold, -0.06em tracking
  "by unfayr"  IBM Plex Mono

USAGE
  Icon corner radius is 22% of its side. Keep clear space of at least
  a quarter of the icon's width around the icon and the lockup.
  In the one-colour versions the white stripes are cut out, so the
  background shows through them.
  Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects or reset the wordmark in
  another font.
