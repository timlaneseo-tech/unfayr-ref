## Past the deadline

Use instead of the first paragraph when the deadline has passed:

- Client: "The review date for {asset_name} was {deadline_day}. Could you let us know when you'll be able to look at it, so we can plan the next steps?"
- Internal: "{asset_name} review was due {deadline_day}. Can you get comments to me by {new_date}, or tell me if you're passing on this round?"
