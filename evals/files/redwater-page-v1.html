Fall Release Party at Redwater.

Join us Saturday, October 24 for the first pour of Copper Hollow Amber, our fall seasonal. Doors at 4 pm, live music from 6.

What to expect

Fresh Copper Hollow on draft, three guest food trucks in the beer garden, and the Mill Street Four playing from 6 to 9 pm. Kids and dogs welcome until 7.

Tickets

General admission is $15 and includes your first pint and a commemorative glass. Tickets are limited to 250.
